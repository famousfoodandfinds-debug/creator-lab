Getting Started
read it once, then go build
How Saxe works
Everything you need before your first script.
The one idea
Saxe does not write about your product. It writes about your buyer, in the exact words real buyers used. You give it reviews and comments, it builds a brief, and the brief feeds every script.
Two things before you paste anything. Your brief is only as good as the material you give it, and what comes out is four angles to take the best parts from, not four scripts to post word for word.
1
Start a product
Sign in and you land on the home screen. First time here? Tap New product to paste your reviews and build your first brief.
2
Paste your material
Add the product name and the listing, then the part that matters most, the buyer's own words.
The fuel
Customer reviews
Your whole brief is built from these. Paste a mix. The happy reviews give the emotion and desire, and the critical ones (3-star and below) are where the objections come from.
Where objections live
Competitor comments
Paste comments from competitor or viral videos. This is where most objections come from, especially when your reviews are all positive.
On your phone, screenshot the comments and upload them. On a computer, paste or drag the screenshot straight in. Saxe reads either.
3
Build the brief
Tap Build brief. Saxe reads everything you pasted and writes it up: who the buyer is, what they feel and want, the pains they are trying to solve, and the objections holding them back.
⚠ An empty objections section means you only pasted good news. Objections come from critical reviews and from video comments. Paste only five-star reviews and skip the comments, and Saxe has nothing to work with, so that section comes back thin or empty. Go back, add a few critical reviews and some competitor comments, and build again.
4
Read and correct the brief
The brief is a draft of what buyers said, not the final word. Read it and fix anything you know better than they do, because you know your product and a handful of reviews do not. Every line is editable, and you can revert if you change your mind.
5
Generate scripts
Tap Generate scripts to get four at a time. You have a set number of generations each month that resets on the 1st, and the counter by the button shows how many you have left, so the limit is never a surprise.
6
Take the best parts
These four are different angles on the same product, not four finished scripts. Take the hook from one, the payoff from another, and reword them so they sound like you. That final wording happens in your own notes or editor. When a card is the closest starting point, use Save to scripts to keep it.
7
The rest
Two more tools sit up top: Planner for scheduling what you will post, and Carousels, a studio that art-directs your image posts. They are there whenever you want them.
